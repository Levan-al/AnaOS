# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in The AnaOS Project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, trolling, or personal attacks
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Report issues to `security@anaos.org` (after domain activation) or the maintainers listed in the GitHub account owning the repositories.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for other behaviors they deem inappropriate.
